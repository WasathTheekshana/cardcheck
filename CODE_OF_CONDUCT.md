# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want everyone to be able to take part in this project without being harassed, whatever their age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, education, nationality, appearance, race,
religion or sexual identity and orientation.

We act in ways that keep the community open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy.

## What we expect

Things that help the community:

- being kind and patient, especially with people who are new
- respecting other points of view and experiences
- giving feedback that is specific and useful, and taking it gracefully
- owning our mistakes, apologising to anyone affected and learning from them
- thinking about what is best for the community, and not only for ourselves

Things that are not acceptable:

- sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- harassment in public or private
- publishing someone else's private information, such as an address or email, without permission
- anything else a reasonable person would call unprofessional in a project setting

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit or reject comments,
commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and will explain why when
they do.

This applies in all project spaces, and when someone is officially representing the project in public.

## Reporting

Report abuse or harassment by using GitHub's "Report content" option on the comment or issue, or by contacting the
maintainer through the email on their GitHub profile. All reports are handled privately and fairly.

## Enforcement steps

Maintainers follow these steps, and may skip ahead if the behaviour is serious:

1. **Correction.** A private note explaining what happened and why it was out of line. A public apology may be
   asked for.
2. **Warning.** A formal warning, with a set period during which there is no interaction with the people involved.
3. **Temporary ban.** A time-limited ban from the project's spaces.
4. **Permanent ban.** For a pattern of violations or serious harassment.
